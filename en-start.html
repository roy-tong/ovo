From first use to ongoing delegation, the state stays clear

You do not need to watch every tool call. You should see business progress, evidence, external waits and the recovery path.

DelegateState the goal, delivery and constraints
PreflightDiscover data, tools, authorization and environment
ExecuteKeep working and checkpoint results
EscalateInterrupt only for necessary authorization or decisions
Deliver & ReuseDeliver, review and continue the next cycle
Running / Waiting ExternalExecuting, or waiting for a user reply, data or external service.
Needs DecisionA decision or authorization is needed; unrelated work can continue.
Partial Result / CompletedDeliver the useful part first, or complete against the task’s acceptance.
Unable to CompleteExplain why work cannot continue and provide a real recovery path.
