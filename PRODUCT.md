# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, so the same source can deploy to GitHub Pages without a build step or runtime dependency. The user asked for a complete replan and unified design; the implementation route was left to the agent.

## Users

- Product managers, founders and researchers who need real evidence before committing product effort.
- Independent developers, open-source maintainers and small teams who need ongoing growth operations.
- AI teams, engineering leaders, finance and procurement who need independent evidence about agent spend and accepted outcomes.
- Human visitors and general-purpose agents evaluating which professional capability to use next.

## Product Purpose

OVO is one product family with three AI-native business-delivery services. Askovo discovers and validates real demand, Growovo carries out delegated growth operations, and Verovo independently measures agent economics. Success means a visitor can understand which product fits a real task, inspect the evidence, and start that task without learning three different websites or internal runtimes.

## Positioning

A general agent can plan, write and operate software. OVO supplies what generic reasoning cannot obtain by itself: real research participants and evidence, persistent operating relationships and receipts, and independent supplier/task economics. The website's job is to make that professional service supply legible and selectable, not to present three disconnected software homepages.

## Operating Context

- Three independently installable prerelease candidates: Askovo 2.25.1rc1, Growovo 0.23.1rc2 and Verovo 0.20.1rc1, with CLI, MCP and Agent Skill entry points.
- Three flagship service groups: Opportunity Research / Demand Validation / Product Intelligence; Managed Growth / First Users / Distribution Intelligence; Agent Spend Healthcheck / Independent Bill Audit / Agent Economics Optimization.
- Real-task evidence and synthetic local examples are shown separately. Current capability and future validation are different states.
- Existing repositories: https://github.com/roy-tong/Askovo, https://github.com/roy-tong/Growovo and https://github.com/roy-tong/Verovo.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One shared navigation, design system, case gallery and task entry for all three products.
- Chinese and English pages share structure and evidence strength.
- No framework, backend, account, tracking or required third-party script is introduced.
- Interactive product selection and case filtering must remain usable with buttons and keyboard input; all essential content remains available without JavaScript.
- No fabricated customer counts, revenue, market share, adoption, savings or external validation.
- Deployment target is a future canonical OVO site; existing public sites have not been migrated in this change.

## Brand Commitments

- One OVO family with three products, not three visually unrelated brands.
- Direct, concrete business language: real research, delegated growth work and independent economic intelligence.
- Unified typography, color roles, spacing, controls, evidence labels and page composition.
- Product identity comes from service content and evidence, not from inconsistent layout or visual themes.

## Evidence on Hand

- Askovo: 109,429 deduplicated comments across 8 communities over 24 months changed the product sequence to software/export validation before dedicated hardware. Convenience sample, not users or market share.
- Growovo: 73 posts in 11 hours produced zero new GitHub Stars. The launch did not use the Growovo ledger that night and is not evidence that X never works.
- Verovo: public measurement across 110+ repositories produced 65+ evidence-backed findings in 5 recurring accounting problem classes. This is technical evidence, not customer savings.
- Verovo: a USD 4.50 synthetic billing difference demonstrates pricing-rule correctness, not a customer loss or recovery.
- Engineering: 70 cases with 69 passing replays in the previous verified baseline; local unreleased work is not external adoption.

## Product Principles

- Delegate complete business outcomes, not isolated feature use.
- Show the work, evidence and next action rather than internal implementation vocabulary.
- Preserve independent product use while presenting one coherent family.
- Distinguish observed facts, synthetic examples and future validation in the content itself.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Responsive desktop and mobile layouts with keyboard-operable controls and visible focus states.
- Semantic landmarks, native buttons, skip navigation and reduced-motion support.
- No required JavaScript for essential product, evidence or installation content.
