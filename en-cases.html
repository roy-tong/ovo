Merged upstream fix

100 tokens, no longer counted as 300

In the open-source tool tokendash, three content blocks could each carry the same response total of 100 tokens. Summing them produced 300. The merged correction groups records by message identity and is publicly inspectable. Your agent data deserves the same independent check.
