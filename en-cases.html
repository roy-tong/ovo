Eleven hours of publishing produced zero new Stars

One historical launch sent 73 posts and produced zero new GitHub Stars. It lacked complete audience, content and conversion controls, so it does not prove X never works. It does show why repeating the same volume lacks evidence.

The metric now follows real tasks, authorized execution, qualified interactions, first value, repeat use and production integration.
