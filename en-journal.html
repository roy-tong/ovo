Each note addresses one question: how the mechanism works, where it fails and why the tradeoff matters. Bilingual summaries are provided here; full articles are in English.

Askovo

A research database needs transaction semantics ↗

Research is not a pile of files. Request snapshots, evidence joins, transactions and isolation determine whether counterevidence can safely change an earlier decision.

Askovo

Product research needs a type system, not a confidence score ↗

A real record can still answer the wrong question. Evidence types and formal conclusion gates replace confidence scores that look precise but cannot be reviewed.

Growovo

Exactly-once execution is the wrong goal ↗

Real operations stop, time out and return unknown outcomes. Approval fingerprints, leases, idempotency and independent readback matter more than “exactly once.”

Growovo

A scheduler that knows when not to send ↗

Continuous execution is not sending on schedule. Eligibility, freshness, budgets, content age and stop conditions determine when the right action is no action.

Verovo

Deterministic replay for AI charges, with the unknowns intact ↗

Deterministic recalculation does not turn unknowns into zero. Event identity, revisions, Decimal arithmetic, content addressing and line-level disposition make charges replayable.

Verovo

A vendor export is not buyer evidence ↗

A supplier export describes one control domain. Independent buyer verification also needs the result observer, source and actual business acceptance.

Verovo

How to optimize AI agent spend without confusing cost with value ↗

Lower spend is not automatically better economics. Cost per accepted result, tool-replacement experiments and quality gates determine whether an optimization actually helped.
