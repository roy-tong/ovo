AskovoDemand research

Build less on guesswork.

Before you invest, find out what people actually need. Askovo turns comments, interviews and public material into demand evidence, alternatives and counterexamples, so you know what to pursue and what to drop.

A general agent can draft a research plan. Askovo goes further by bringing back outside evidence, so your decision does not depend on assumptions in a prompt.

VerovoEconomic verification

You can only optimize spend you understand.

Verovo finds duplicates, anomalies and inefficient work in usage logs and supplier bills, then connects charges to tasks and accepted outcomes. Replace a tool, change the workflow or keep the current setup based on evidence.

You see not only what was charged, but what each accepted result cost and which charges still need inspection.
