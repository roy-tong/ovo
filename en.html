AskovoReal research

Why invest before you know the demand is real?

Askovo lets an agent combine opportunity research, demand validation and ongoing product intelligence. You get source evidence, counterexamples and a way to reopen the decision when new facts arrive—not just a polished report.

A general agent can design a study. Askovo adds the part that prompts cannot invent: real users, market evidence and experiment records that test the decision against the outside world.

GrowovoContinuous operations

Who keeps the customer conversation moving after launch?

Growovo handles market listening, replies, follow-up, first use and distribution reviews inside clear authorization. You keep the goals, accounts and key decisions while agents take over repetitive operating work.

Send volume is not growth. Growovo records action receipts, qualified interaction, first use and voluntary reuse separately. Seven-day delegation and real activation still require their own evidence.

VerovoEconomic verification

Was the agent spend actually worth it?

Verovo independently recalculates logs, bills and accepted outcomes so you can compare tools, models and execution styles on cost and quality. You see not only discrepancies, but the full cost of each accepted result.

A supplier export is not buyer evidence. Verovo separates estimates, confirmed charges, bill amounts, control coverage and realized savings. Anything it cannot verify stays unknown.

LESS STRUCTURE, MORE REALITY

Agents choose the path. OVO supplies the real world.

You should not have to learn a proprietary workflow first. A host agent can compose data, services, execution, durable state and deterministic constraints while OVO preserves evidence and business boundaries. “The model says it finished” is not acceptance.

Every task should expose progress, external waits, partial results and a way to resume. The benchmark is not feature count: it is better first-task delivery than a native agent, less human effort and a reason for users to come back.
