Who follows up after launch?

Define an audience and the actions you authorize. Growovo screens opportunities, tracks replies and keeps the next follow-up attached to each conversation.

A scheduler manages publication. Growovo follows the conversation afterward, recording delivery, replies and first use separately.

Do these charges add up?

Match usage records to a supplier bill, recalculate line items at the applicable rates and trace spending to accepted tasks.

A usage dashboard shows reported spend. Verovo recalculates from buyer-side records and flags lines that still need evidence.
